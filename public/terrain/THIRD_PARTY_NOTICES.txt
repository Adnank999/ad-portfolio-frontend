Dragon model

"Demon Dragon Full Texture" by endlessvoidmc
https://sketchfab.com/endlessvoidmc
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/demon-dragon-full-texture-19035a72cdcb4abfa2c161de32823e6b
License identified by the supplied GLB metadata: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The supplied GLB is unmodified. This prototype creates two runtime skeleton clones,
adjusts material tint/roughness, plays the supplied flying animation, adds a jaw
pose and mouth sockets, and places the dragons on procedural flight paths.
Fire, ice, terrain-contact effects and particle shaders are prototype additions.

Draco decoder

The unmodified glTF-targeted draco_decoder.wasm and draco_wasm_wrapper.js are
copied from the installed Three.js 0.170.0 examples distribution.

https://github.com/google/draco
